# NBA Article Cover Image Prompts

## Prompt Formula

Use this structure:

`Editorial sports cover for [Team A] vs [Team B], [main visual metaphor], [team color palette without logos], dramatic NBA arena lighting, hardwood court texture, dynamic composition, space for Chinese headline text, high contrast, magazine cover style, 16:9`

Add a negative prompt when the tool supports it:

`no official logos, no readable brand marks, no exact player likeness, no distorted hands, no extra text unless requested`

## Visual Concepts

| Scenario | Prompt angle |
| --- | --- |
| Star duel | Two opposing basketball silhouettes facing off under split-color arena lights |
| Defensive battle | Locked-down half court, pressure shadows, low-angle rim protection visual |
| Pace matchup | Motion blur transition attack, streaking court lines, scoreboard glow |
| Playoff pressure | Close-up hardwood, confetti-like dust, intense spotlight, elimination atmosphere |
| Upset alert | Underdog color beam cutting through dominant team's color field |
| Injury uncertainty | Empty spotlight over one side, bench silhouette, tactical board overlay |

## Chinese Headline Space

If the cover needs Chinese headline placement, ask the image generator for:

- clean negative space in the upper third or left third;
- no generated Chinese characters inside the image unless exact typography will be added later;
- strong subject separation so text remains readable on mobile thumbnails.

## Safer Brand Treatment

Use city and color cues instead of protected marks:

- "green and cream Boston-inspired palette" rather than official Celtics logo;
- "purple and gold Los Angeles-inspired palette" rather than Lakers logo;
- "navy and gold Bay Area-inspired palette" rather than Warriors logo.
