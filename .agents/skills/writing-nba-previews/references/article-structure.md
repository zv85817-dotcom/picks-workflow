# NBA Preview Article Structures

## Standard Chinese Sports Article

Use when the user asks for a public-facing preview or WeChat-style article.

1. Headline: include matchup and tension, not just team names.
2. Opening hook: why this game matters tonight.
3. Situation: standings, recent form, injuries, rest, travel, and season-series context.
4. Three watch points:
   - Core star matchup or primary creation source.
   - Tactical hinge such as spacing, rim protection, switchability, pace, offensive rebounding, or bench minutes.
   - Psychological/stakes angle such as revenge game, playoff seeding, home-court urgency, or fatigue.
5. Prediction: winner, likely game script, score range, confidence level.
6. Closing line: a sharp final sentence that frames the central question.

## Betting-Style Preview

Use only when the user asks for spread, total, moneyline, picks, or betting analysis.

1. State the current line and source/time checked.
2. Explain market context without assuming the market is correct.
3. Identify injury/rest variables that could move the line.
4. Compare matchup edges to the spread or total.
5. Give a lean or pass recommendation with risk notes.
6. Avoid certainty language: no "lock", "sure win", or guaranteed profit framing.

## Short Social Post

Use when the user asks for a brief preview.

- 1 hook sentence.
- 3 bullet watch points.
- 1 prediction sentence.
- 1 cover prompt or thumbnail idea.

## Playoff Preview

Emphasize adjustments and repeat matchups:

- Previous game counters and likely coaching responses.
- Rotation changes and matchup hunting.
- Foul trouble, rebounding, shot diet, and half-court creation.
- Home/away role-player shooting splits only if sourced and relevant.
